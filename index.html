Your Data. Your Control.

TeacherPro runs in your browser and communicates with supported services to perform the actions you request. Credentials and preferences are stored in Chrome extension storage; this is not an encrypted credential vault.

  • ✓ Classroom content is not sent to our licensing service for validation
  • ✓ License validation uses Cloudflare and Lemon Squeezy
  • ✓ Validation sends your license key and a random installation ID
  • ✓ License records include subscription and activation information

Get Started with TeacherPro

Install the extension, check compatibility, and try the tools you need before subscribing.

1. Check access

For Canvas tools that require API access, provide a permitted Personal Access Token in Settings. Your school's restrictions still apply.

2. Try your workflow

A 7-day Premium trial lets you check the features available to your account. Free tools remain available after the trial.

3. Activate your subscription

Find your license key in your emailed receipt, open Settings > Subscription, enter the key, and select Verify with Server.
