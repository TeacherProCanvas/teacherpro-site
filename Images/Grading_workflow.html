1. Click on Max next to the View Rubric button to automatically maximize the score.

2. The rubric will automatically select the highest option possible. You can still click "View Rubric" if you need to make scoring changes.

3. After clicking Max, the view will automatically focus on the Comment box.

4. Click on Add Greeting, to add a personalized greeting to the feedback. The student's first name will be added automatically, and you can set the initial greeting like "Hello", or "Good Afternoon" in TeacherPro Settings.

5. The student's name is automatically added.

6. Click on Add Signature

7. A customized signature is added to the end of your comments automatically. You can customize the signature in TeacherPro Settings.

8. To save your feedback, click on Save to Library. If you included a custom greeting, it will automatically remove it (e.g. Hello Student,) and leave the rest of the feedback.

9. The next time you visit this same assignment, you will see your feedback shortcut button (Helpful Feedback).

10. Click on HelpfulFeedback

11. It will automatically insert your saved feedback!

12. Click on Submit and you are done with this assigmnment!

13. To go to the next student, click on Next Ungraded at the top. The course will give you an indicator if the next assignment is a "Different Assignment", "Same Assignment", or "New Course."

14. You can click on the Ungraded list to view a list of all ungraded items. The number (6) displayed shows how many ungraded items are left in the list.

15. The queue can be sorted by student, assignment, or submitted date. If you need to manually refresh the list, you can do so here.
