How to Use Course Scheduler

How to Use Course Scheduler

Help Page

Walkthrough

Interactive Tutorial

23 STEPS

1. The first step is to open TeacherPro Dashboard and select your course by clicking Change Course.

2. Use the filter at the top to narrow down your courses, then click on the course and click "Save course".

3. Enter a preset if available and skip to Step 7. Otherwise, enter the Start Date here. This is the first scheduled date, so move it forward if you do not want items scheduled on the first day.

4. Enter the End Date. This marks the last scheduled item. Adjust it if you want items to end before the official course end date, or extend it if the Final occurs later. Add holiday dates between the course end date and Final if needed.

5. Enter any holidays or blackout dates you don't want items scheduled. If you have a gap between the Course End Date and the Final Date, include those dates here so items are not scheduled on those dates.

6. If you want to save the dates for later, enter a Preset Name here and click "Save as New Preset."

7. When you are satisfied with your dates, click Load Course Items

8. Large checkboxes represent Modules and small ones represent course items. Columns show whether each item is scheduled and published. Special scheduling is for Honors projects or assignments you want distributed evenly across the course.

9. Click Expand or Collapse Module buttons for easier selection.

10. Use the filters to find specific items in the course. You can also se the checkboxes to show or hide items by type.
Note: Summit Courses Quizzes are considered "Assignment" type. Use the Omit box "quiz" to hide these instead of the checkbox.

11. Select items individually or use the buttons. The purple button selects visible items only, not the module. If a module is unpublished, its items are inaccessible even if published. The blue button selects the module and all visible items.

12. Use the green buttons to either Schedule Items or Publish Items

13. Use the Red buttons to either Unschedule Items or Unpublish Items.
(NOTE: If you unpublish a module, ALL items in it will be inaccessible to students even if the items themselves are published!)

14. Use Clear filters or Deselect All Visible to reset your selections.

15. If your course is designed for students to complete one module per day, use "Preview: Schedule by Module" otherwise, use "Preview: Schedule by item."

16. If needed, use the "Set Date" in the preview table to adjust the schedule.

17. You can also drag and drop items between days.

18. Use the arrow buttons to arrange items in the desired order.

19. Check that the course ends on the correct date. Adjust the Final by if needed. (Note: if a Schedule Day box is left empty, it disappears and cannot be restored unless you click Preview again. Adding extra days is not yet possible.)

20. When you are satisfied with your schedule, click Apply Changes.

21. Enter a course prefix for the calendar then click Save & Apply. It should briefly identify the course, such as "MATH1" or "HST101".
With Schedule by Item, it shows “[prefix] - Due Today.”
With Schedule by Module, it shows “[prefix] - [module name].”

22. Type HST101 in Calendar Entry Prefix

23. That's it. You're done.

How to Apply Temporary Zeros

Step 1: Set Date Filter

Set a cutoff date or date range to find assignments, quizzes, and discussions due by that date.

Step 2: Load Assignments

Click "Load Assignments" to find all items matching your date filter.

Step 3: Filter and Select

Use the search and type filters to find specific items. Check the boxes for items where you want to apply temporary zeros.

Step 4: Apply Temporary Zeros

Once you've selected items, click "Apply Temporary Zeros to Selected" to grade students with no submission.

Tip

Click the "Quick Tour" button for an interactive walkthrough!

Temporary Zeros - Multi-Course

Apply temporary zeros across multiple courses using the same workflow as the single-course tool: set date filters, load assignments, select items, and apply.

How to Manage Navigation Tabs

Step 1: Select Courses

Click on one or more courses in the list. Hold Ctrl/Cmd to select multiple courses at once.

Step 2: Choose a Navigation Tab

Select the tab you want to modify from the dropdown (e.g., Assignments, Grades, Modules).

Step 3: Apply Visibility

Click "Make Visible in Selected Courses" to show the tab or "Hide in Selected Courses" to hide it.

Step 4: Review Results

The results will appear below showing the status of your changes.

Click the "Quick Tour" button for an interactive walkthrough!

How to Manage Announcements

Step 1: Load Courses

Click "Load Courses" to fetch all your courses. Use search and omit filters to narrow down the list, then select the courses you want to work with.

Step 2: Choose an Action

Click "Load Announcements" to view existing posts, or "Post Announcement..." to create a new one.

Step 3: For New Posts

Enter a title, optionally set start/end dates, choose Basic or HTML editor, and compose your message.

Step 4: Post or Delete

Post new announcements to selected courses or delete existing ones in bulk after filtering and selecting them.

Walkthrough

Interactive Tutorial

21 STEPS

1. The first step is to open TeacherPro Dashboard - Google Chrome and click FIND A TOOL

2. Type "Announcements" in FIND A TOOL

3. Click Announcement Manager

4. Click Load Courses

5. Use the Search Bar to filter your courses.

6. Click Select All Visible

7. If you want to edit or delete existing announcements, click Load Announcements

8. Use the search bar to locate the announcement(s) you want to edit or delete.

9. If you want to post one announcement to your selected courses, click Post Single Announcement.

10. Type in your announcement details in Announcement title

11. If you want to use an HTML code, use the toggle here.

12. Paste in your HTML code.

13. Click Post to Selected Course.

14. If you want to post multiple announcements to your selected courses, click Post Multiple Announcements.

15. Click Post Multiple Announcements

16. Copy the template here, paste into Excel and add your title, and html code.

17. Then, paste your entire completed table here.

18. Click Parse Table

19. Scroll down and Click Preview.

20. The HTML code will load and display your announcement. Click "Post Parsed Rows to Selected Courses" to post your announcements to all selected courses.

21. That's it. You're done.

How to Reset Calendar

Step 1: Click Preview

Click the Preview button to see all calendar events in your course.

Step 2: Filter and Select Events

Use "Filter by Title" to find specific events. Use "Omit items with Title" to exclude certain events. Select the events you want to delete using the checkboxes.

Step 3: Click Confirm & Delete

Once you've selected all events to delete, click the "Confirm & Delete" button.

Step 4: Confirm in Popup

A confirmation popup will appear. Click "Yes, Delete Events" to complete the action. This action cannot be undone.

Tip

Click the "Quick Tour" button for an interactive walkthrough!

How to Reset Dates

Step 1: Click Preview

Click the Preview button to see all graded items with dates in your course.

Step 2: Filter and Select Items

Use "Filter by Title" to find specific items. Use "Omit items with Title" to exclude certain items. Select the items you want to reset dates for using the checkboxes.

Step 3: Click Confirm & Reset

Once you've selected all items to reset, click the "Confirm & Reset" button.

Step 4: Confirm in Popup

A confirmation popup will appear. Click "Yes, Reset Dates" to complete the action. This action cannot be undone.

How to Match Rubrics

Step 1: Load Assignments

Click "Load Assignments" to find all graded items (assignments and discussions) in your course.

Step 2: Filter Results

Use the search and omit fields to narrow down the list to the items you're interested in.

Step 3: Review Matches

The tool automatically suggests rubric matches based on similar naming patterns (e.g., "1.03" matches both a rubric and an assignment).

Step 4: Apply Rubrics

Review the suggested rubric associations and click "Apply Rubric Settings" to attach the rubrics to your assignments.

SpeedGrader Enhancements

Walkthrough

Interactive Tutorial

14 STEPS

1. From your Canvas Dashboard, click the plus sign on the grading widget, then click Start grading

2. OPTIONAL: If you use rubrics, you can click Max to set all rubric criteria to full points.

3. OPTIONAL: If you have added a signature in Settings, you can click Add Signature to insert your signature at the bottom of the comment box.

4. OPTIONAL: Does your student use a preferred name? You can click Set Nickname to have Canvas use the nickname when inserting saved feedback or when using "Add Greeting".

5. OPTIONAL: Want to personalize your feedback? Click Add Greeting to insert "[Greetings] [Student's name/nickname].
You can customize your greeting in settings, for example: "Hola" or "Hello".

6. Optional: Want to save commonly used feedback for next time?
Click Save to Library!

7. To save your feedback, enter a "Tag" in the box. For example: "blank".

8. Want to save this feedback for other assignments in the same course? Click Show.

9. Then, click Get Assignments.

10. Choose your assignments from the list You can use the filter boxes to refine your search. Then, click Save

11. To use your saved feedback, refresh the page, and your "Blank" button will appear here.

12. When you have finished grading this assignment, you can click Next Ungraded to go to the next assignment.

13. If you want to see the list of ungraded items, you can click the Ungraded list, or, if you only want to grade the same assignment you can click the Lock icon stay on the current assignment.

14. That's it. You're done.

Widget - Bulk Exempt or Excuse (Single Student)

Walkthrough

Interactive Tutorial

9 STEPS

1. The first step is to open your Canvas Dashboard - and click + on the New Enrollments Widget.

2. Click Manage New Enrollments

3. If you want to check to see if a student has already been exempted, you can click the student's name to to be taken to that student's gradebook. Otherwise, go to the next step.

4. To exempt/excuse a SINGLE student, click the date.

5. The student will automatically be selected from the course list. The date will also be auto-filled. You only need to click Load Assignments

6. Click Select All to select the assignments due on or before the detected date.

7. Click Apply Exemptions

8. Verify that the student and exemptions are correct, then click Apply.

9. All done!

Widget - Bulk Exempt or Excuse (By Course)

Walkthrough

Interactive Tutorial

12 STEPS

1. The first step is to open your Canvas Dashboard - and click + on the New Enrollments Widget.

2. Click Manage New Enrollments

3. Scroll down to the "Enrollment history by course" section.

4. Click the "New Only" box.

5. Optional - Click the New column to sort.

6. To exempt or excuse ALL new students, click Exempt NEW students.

7. The students will automatically be selected.

8. The date will be auto-filled. You only need to click Load Assignments.

9. Click Select All

10. Click Apply Exemptions

11. Verify the exemptions and students, then click Apply.

12. Tutorial Finished
